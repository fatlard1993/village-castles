# Village Castles

A Fabric mod that adds castles to the world - every one generated by code, none pasted from a
template. Two lineages share one architectural grammar:

- **Ancient castles** rise in the wilderness where the land itself calls for them: a terrain
  classifier reads each candidate site and either names a landform or rejects it. Plateau →
  rim-walled citadel, hilltop → terraced ringfort, cliff face → a hold built against and into the
  rock, island → sea fort with a boat-sized sea gate, headland → promontory fort behind one
  massive neck wall, ridge → an elongated wall-hold with a gatehouse tunnel, jagged peak → a lone
  beacon eyrie. One civilization's deepslate-and-slate masonry everywhere, dressed in a regional
  accent stone (tuff, cut sandstone, mud brick, mossy stone brick, calcite), weathered by a
  per-site condition roll: ruined 50% / crumbling 35% / weathered-but-standing 15%. The rarer,
  more intact finds carry the best loot and the heaviest undead garrison (skeletons; strays in the
  cold, husks in the desert); ruins offer archaeology digs instead.
- **Villager castles** are what villagers raised in imitation of the ruins they found: the same
  grammar - keeps with corner pilasters and string courses, merlon-and-slab coping, gatehouse
  arches - spoken in the biome's own timber and stone, new-built, furnished, torch-lit, and
  garrisoned by villagers who spawn at their beds. They attach to village edges at worldgen
  (~20% of villages; sizes small watch keep / medium walled fort / large castle seat at
  40/35/25), and with [Village Builder](../village-builder) installed, a growing village can
  raise one itself.

Because everything is generated in place against a sampled height field, the terrain shapes
every build: walls foot on their own ground, battlements level off over uneven footings, and no
two castles are the same while all of them are visibly kin.

## Stairs you can walk up

A keep floors its whole interior every six blocks, and the spiral stair climbs straight through
them. Placing a tread only replaced the one block it landed on, which left the slab directly over
your head intact - so every storey meant stopping to mine the ceiling. The stair now cuts its own
stairwell as it is placed: two blocks above each tread, which is exactly a player's headroom. The
hole is helical, following the walk rather than opening the floor out into something to fall down.

## Commands

All run by a player. `ancient`, `ancientscan` and `villager` need operator permission (level 2);
`list` and `help` are open to everyone.

- `/village-castles ancient <landform> [condition [seed]]` - raise an ancient castle at your
  position through the real worldgen pipeline; the same seed reproduces the same castle
- `/village-castles ancientscan` - run the landform classifier where you stand and print every
  metric plus the verdict (the threshold-tuning instrument)
- `/village-castles villager <size> [biome [seed]]` - raise a villager castle at your position
  (biome defaults to the one you stand in)
- `/village-castles list` - the vocabulary (landforms, conditions, sizes, biomes)
- `/village-castles help` - the syntax above

**Landforms**: `plateau`, `hilltop`, `cliff_face`, `island`, `headland`, `ridge`, `eyrie`
**Conditions**: `ruined`, `crumbling`, `weathered` (default: rolled from the seed); `pristine`, the
villager castles' undecayed state, is also accepted
**Sizes**: `small`, `medium`, `large` · **Biomes**: `plains`, `desert`, `savanna`, `taiga`, `snowy`

## How it generates

A castle is drawn once into a deterministic block plan (an ordered position→state map plus
chests, spawners, and dig sites) from a seed and a serialized terrain sample, and each chunk
writes the subset that lands inside it - so a castle spanning many chunks assembles seamlessly
in any generation order. Decay happens while drawing, never as a post-pass: erosion bites and
collapse heights come from position hashes every chunk computes identically. Villager castles
run the same machinery at condition PRISTINE.

Wilderness placement: `village-castles:ancient_castle` generates at roughly woodland-mansion
rarity (structure-set spacing 48/20, thinned further by classifier rejection) and refuses sites
within 8 chunks of a predicted village - the ancients predate the towns. Village attachment runs
after jigsaw assembly with a per-side terrain survey, and a grounds piece underfills the
footprint and spawns the garrison at bed heads.

## Village Builder integration

When [Village Builder](../village-builder) is installed, three procedural entries (small /
medium / large) register through its `BuildPlanProvider` API: at build time the provider runs
the villager-castle designer against the village's own terrain, biome, and a chosen facing, and
Village Builder places the resulting plan. All three share one limit group
(`village-castles:castle`, max 1), and a census seeder walks a village's structure pieces so a
village that *generated* with a castle is never offered another. The integration is wired
reflectively - either mod runs alone; an older Village Builder logs one warning and offers no
castles.

## Village Quests integration

When [Village Quests](../village-quests) is installed, a village that has a castle talks about it,
and only such a village: masons, fletchers, farmers, librarians and toolsmiths (and occasionally
anyone) ask for small errands for the keep - stone for the wall in the local material, arrows,
bread, a book, iron for the gate, torches - and several professions have a question about the
wall, the keep, the golem or the one raid, offered some of the time. Where an ancient castle can
be located from the village, the cleric, mason and librarian can speak of it, and a trusted
cleric may send you out to it for a bone. Also wired reflectively; either mod runs alone.
